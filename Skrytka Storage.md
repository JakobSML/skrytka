## How it works

Three steps, and you only do the first one.

1. ### Choose your space

   Pick the square metres and how many pickups and returns you want each year.
2. ### We collect your things

   A driver comes to your door in the agreed window and labels every box with an ID.
3. ### Find it in your account

   Each box has a notes field. Write what is inside, search later, and request a return in two taps.

## Questions

Draft answers. Replace them with your real terms before launch.

How do you collect my things?

You choose a time window in the app. A driver picks up from your door and brings boxes to the storage hub in Śródmieście. Each box gets a numbered label.

Is it safe?

The hub is dry, heated above freezing, monitored and insured. Add your own insurance details here once you have a provider.

Can I change the size later?

Yes. You can grow or shrink in half-metre steps. The change applies from the next billing period.

What can't I store?

Food, fuel, gas cylinders, chemicals, anything flammable, and anything alive.

What happens if I cancel?

We return everything to you on a final trip, then close the subscription.
